## Learned this session

- **Brief every reviewer with the principle and the scope.** "Another process changing entries while the app acts is
  out of scope (CONTEXT.md, Artifact removal; BACKLOG #119)." Without that scope line, reviews drift into hardening
  against concurrent-process races and never converge (ticket 19, round 2).
- **When review rounds keep finding new counterexamples to the same proof, remove what needs proving.** This
  happened twice: ticket 09 (grouping rows to share ownership → act per raw row) and ticket 10 (the two-rename
  fallback with a witness → exchange or refuse). In both, the code shrank and the reviews converged.
- **OpenAI's content filter stops review briefs** that contain words like "try to break", "TOCTOU" or "defeat". Phrase
  briefs as correctness and data-integrity reviews.
- **State a merge bar per round:** blocking means a P0 or P1, or a violation of the principle; everything else goes to
  a named later ticket. It keeps rounds short.
- **Whole-release reviews catch interactions** that per-ticket reviews can't see. Here: Kimi's folder correction ×
  per-folder rows × Disable.
- **Children never commit under `.scratch/`.** Their Comments entries stay in their worktrees. The orchestrator writes
  one stand-in entry per ticket at merge.
