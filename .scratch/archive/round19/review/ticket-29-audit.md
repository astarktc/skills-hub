## Findings and release judgment

### Closed: ticket-27 F2 (P1)

`src/components/ui/sonner.tsx:76` and `src/components/ui/layer-stack.ts:204` now put the single polite toaster outside the inert app root and exempt its host. Independently observed refusal and outcome AX exposure under both modal placements, working pointer action, focus return and stacked dismissal. **No audit fix needed.**

### Accepted: deliberate foreground-toast focus under a modal

`node_modules/sonner/dist/index.mjs:1046–1127` confirms that the hotkey focuses the OL, Escape only collapses its expansion without consuming the event, and leaving the toaster restores the previously focused element. Runtime proofs establish that this previous element remains in the active dialog. This is not a strict “focus can never leave the popup by any means” promise: both the documented hotkey and the expressly required pointer action can enter the foreground toast. It is **not** an accidental Tab escape into the app, a lost-focus path, or an inescapable loop. Keeping the deliberate shortcut also gives keyboard users access to View report without closing the dialog. Accept for 1.3.0; no modal-dependent hotkey suppression is warranted by these results.

### Residual risks, not newly found defects

- AX presence proves eligibility for assistive-technology announcement, **not measured VoiceOver speech** or native Tauri/WebKit behavior. The operator's native screen-reader smoke remains useful; no claim of a full WCAG audit.
- Ticket-27 F3 / BACKLOG #80 (toast obscures header controls and hover pauses it) is unchanged and remains a separate follow-up. This audit does not waive or close it.
- The host intentionally assumes a DOM, just like the app entry. A future SSR port would need a separate supported rendering contract.
