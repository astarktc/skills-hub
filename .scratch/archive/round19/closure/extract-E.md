   `mutation_guard::serialized` and refuse it while any target is symlinked. Hiding Browse… until it is redesigned is an
   alternative.

   (`issues/28-…md:26,115`, "not filed")? Recommended: **yes, one issue linking the fix approach (`cd33e1e` + `3905da3`).** It
   costs nothing, and the bug is still upstream as of their `1647138`. Otherwise drop it by name.
3. **E6 + #90.** Merge them into one "fixture parity" item? Recommended: **yes.** Both are fixture-only coverage holes with the
   same cause (the fixture diverges from the backend). One item keeps them findable.
